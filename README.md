# F1 Start Reaction Game

A fast-paced, browser-based reaction test inspired by the F1 starting gantry.

## How to Play
1. Open the game in your browser: [https://jshhomelab.github.io/f1-reaction-game/](https://jshhomelab.github.io/f1-reaction-game/)
2. Click the screen or press the **Spacebar** to start the sequence.
3. Wait for the five red lights to turn on and then go out.
4. **React as fast as you can** the moment the lights turn off!
5. Be careful—clicking too early results in a "Jump Start."

## Features
- **Authentic Feel:** Realistic 5-light gantry sequence with randomized reaction timing.
- **Performance Rating:** Get instant feedback on your reaction speed (e.g., *F1 Legend* vs *Safety Car*).
- **History & Stats:** Automatically tracks your attempts, calculates your session average, and highlights your personal best.
- **Keyboard Controls:** Use your spacebar for higher precision.
- **Audio Cues:** Authentic rising pitch beeps for lights on, and a sharp "GO" tone (can be muted).
- **Persistent:** Your stats and preferences (like mute status) are saved in your browser.
- **Export:** Easily copy your statistics to share with friends.

## Technical Details
- Built with HTML5, CSS3 (for styling and animations), and vanilla JavaScript.
- Uses the **Web Audio API** for synthesized sound effects.
- Stores history locally using **localStorage**.
- Hosted on **GitHub Pages**.
